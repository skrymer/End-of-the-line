# Post template

`post.md` is the structure every End of the Line post follows, based on the Cleveland post.

## Structure

1. **All aboard at Roma Street** – every trip starts here
2. **Along the line** – the ride and the view from the window
3. **Arriving at <Destination>** – first impression of the last stop
4. **What to do** – one section per thing worth doing, named after it
5. **Heading home** – the train back
6. **At the end of the line** – a quick list of what's there, the same bullets every time

## Style

- Keep it short: one or two sentences per paragraph, then a photo.
- Write as a trip taken, in the past tense, first person.
- Focus on what there is to do at the end of the line. The ride is the lead-in.
- Sum up the stops in one line instead of listing them.
- Only state what you saw or checked. Leave out guesses.
- Give every photo alt text describing what it shows.

## Photos

- Name files after what they show, e.g. `marina-clouds.jpg`.
- Strip location metadata before committing, as the repo is public.
- Pick a cover that says "train", e.g. the platform at Roma Street or the destination sign.
